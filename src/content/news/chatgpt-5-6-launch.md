## Sol

The marquee variant, **Sol**, represents OpenAI's top-tier reasoning powerhouse. Engineered with expanded context window dynamics and dynamic chain-of-thought routing, Sol achieves unprecedented scores on complex logic, multi-language coding benchmarks, and advanced mathematical proofs. Early benchmarks indicate a 34% reduction in hallucination rates compared to its predecessor, alongside noticeable improvements in following intricate, multi-step system prompts.

## Terra and Luna

Meanwhile, **Terra** serves as the balanced workhorse optimized for general conversation, creative drafting, and daily enterprise tasks, delivering a 2x throughput speedup at reduced token cost. For edge computing and real-time voice applications, **Luna** operates with extreme efficiency, boasting sub-100ms response latency. Users can seamlessly switch between variants directly within the interface or route requests dynamically via API parameters.
